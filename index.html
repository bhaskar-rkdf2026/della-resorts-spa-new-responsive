Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to book an appointment in advance? +

Yes — we recommend reserving your preferred time and therapist ahead of arrival, especially during peak resort season, to guarantee availability.

How early should I arrive? +

We recommend arriving 15 to 20 minutes prior to your scheduled treatment time to enjoy our relaxation lounge and complete your consultation.

What should I wear during my treatment? +

We provide comfortable robes, slippers, and disposable undergarments. You may wear whatever makes you feel most comfortable.

What is the cancellation policy? +

Cancellations made at least 4 hours in advance will incur no charge. Cancellations within 4 hours may be subject to a 50% fee.
